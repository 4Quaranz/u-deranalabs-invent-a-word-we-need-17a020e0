One new English word, defined in a single clear line (72 characters):

> **Appurge** *(n.)*: the quiet relief of deleting an app that stole your hours.

*Appurge* is a blend of **app** + **purge**: the feeling every phone owner
knows — the small, quiet relief when you finally delete the app that kept
stealing your hours. Sayable (ap-PURGE), spellable, memorable, and it
sounds like English grew it.
